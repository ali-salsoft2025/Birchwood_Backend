Privacy Policy
The Birchwood Academy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how The Birchwood Academy collects, uses, and shares personal information in the parent app, the teacher app, and the school admin panel. Please read it carefully. The current version is always available in Settings in the parent app and the teacher app.


1. Who this policy applies to

Parents and guardians use the parent app. Students do not have a login of their own.

Teachers use the teacher app only after the school has created and activated their account.

School administrators use the admin panel to manage classes, people, and school records.

If you use any of these services, this policy applies to you. For a parent account, it also applies to the students linked to that account.


2. Parent accounts

A parent may create an account in the parent app, or a school administrator may create it.

We ask for the father’s name, the mother’s name, an email address, a phone number, and a password. An address and photographs are optional.

Each parent account uses one email address. If the school marks the account inactive, that person cannot sign in until the school activates it again.


3. Teacher accounts

A teacher cannot create a teacher account. A school administrator creates it in the admin panel.

The school may keep the teacher’s name, email address, telephone numbers, home address, date and place of birth, photograph, biography, education, employment history, and assigned class. The administrator chooses a starting password, or the system generates one and shows it once, so the administrator can give it to the teacher.

The account remains pending until an administrator activates it. A pending account cannot sign in. Once it is active, the teacher signs in with the email address and password on the account, and may change the password in the teacher app. A teacher cannot register another teacher account from the app, and cannot activate or reassign their own account.


4. Student records

A student does not register and is not given a password. A school administrator creates the student record.

The record may include the roll number, term, name, age, date of birth, photograph, class, and notes about allergies, fears, health conditions, and a short summary. Those notes are used so staff know what matters during the school day.


5. How a student is linked to a parent

The school holds the student record. It appears in a parent account only after it is linked.

A student can be linked to one parent account at a time. A parent account can be linked to more than one student.

There are two ways to make the link.

The school assigns the parent. When an administrator creates or edits a student, they may select a parent account. The student then appears on that account. If the administrator later selects a different parent, the student moves to the new account and is removed from the previous one. If the administrator removes the parent, the student is no longer linked.

The parent links an unassigned student. If no parent is linked yet, the signed-in parent may add the student by entering the roll number and date of birth. Both must match the record the school created. If they do not match, the student is not linked. If another parent is already linked, the request is declined. A parent cannot move a student off another parent account.

Teachers do not assign students to parents. A teacher sees the students in the class the school has assigned to them.

Please share a student’s roll number and date of birth only with the person who should hold the parent account. Those two details are what allow an unassigned student to be linked.


6. Information we collect

Account details
The information described in sections 2, 3, and 4. Passwords are stored in a form that cannot be read back. We do not keep the password as plain text.

School records
Attendance, including present, absent, leave, late arrival, early pickup, and any reason given. Homework, notices, the timetable, and the school calendar. Assessment results after the school publishes them. Fee vouchers for a student, including the amount, month, due date, and whether the fee is marked paid.

Class posts
Text, photographs, and video published for a student or a class. People who are allowed to see a post may like it or comment on it.

Messages
Conversations between a parent and a teacher about a linked student. A message may include text, a photograph, a file, or a voice recording.

Support
The subject and messages in a support request, and the student or class named in that request, if any.

Password reset
If you ask to reset your password, we email a short code to the address on your account. We keep that code only until you use it or it expires.

On your device
The app keeps your sign-in session so you can stay signed in. It may also keep posts or chat files you choose to save, and the notification setting you choose in Settings.

We do not collect your location.


7. Why we use this information

We use it to run the school: to keep enrolment records, organise classes, record attendance, share homework and results, manage fees, and let parents, teachers, and the school office communicate.

We use health notes so staff can take account of allergies, fears, and conditions at school.

We use your email address to sign you in and, when you ask, to send a password-reset code.

We do not sell personal information. We do not pass it to advertising networks, and we do not use it to build marketing profiles. Banners shown in the apps are published by the school.


8. Who can see a student’s information

The linked parent can see that student’s profile, class, attendance, homework and notices, published results, fee vouchers, class posts about that student or class, and the conversation with the class teacher. The parent may update the health notes and photograph for their own linked students only.

The class teacher can see the students in their assigned class, and the class tools the school has enabled, such as attendance, homework, results, and class posts. A teacher does not see students from another class, and does not see the full school roll, in the teacher app.

School administrators can see and update student, parent, and teacher records in order to operate the school.

Other parents in the same class may see class posts and class notices. They cannot see another family’s profile, health notes, fees, or private messages.


9. Camera, photographs, and microphone

The parent app may use your photo library when you add an optional profile photograph or choose an image to send.

The teacher app may use the camera, photo library, and microphone when you take or choose a photograph, record a voice message, or add media to a class post.

We use these only when you choose that feature.


10. How long we keep information

We keep student, parent, and teacher records, including attendance, fees, homework, and results, while the person is connected with the school, and for as long afterward as the school needs them for its records.

Notices inside the app are deleted after about 60 days.

Photographs, files, and voice messages stored with a chat on our servers are deleted after about 30 days. A copy you have already saved on your device stays there until you delete it. Clearing a chat in the app does not remove a copy that the other person still has.

When a photograph is replaced or removed, we delete the previous file from our server.


11. How we protect information

Access depends on the type of account. A parent or teacher sign-in cannot open the admin panel.

Student information is limited to the linked parent, the teacher of that student’s class, and school administrators, as set out in section 8.

Parents cannot create or change homework. A teacher may edit or delete homework they created. The teacher who wrote a class post may edit it, and an administrator may remove it. School-wide fee records are available to administrators. A parent sees fee vouchers only for a linked student.


12. Your choices

You may update your profile and change your password in the app. Parents may also update health notes and the photograph of a linked student, and may turn notices on or off in Settings.

To correct a record, unlink a student, request a copy of the information we hold, or ask us to close an account, contact the school through Support. We may keep information we still need for enrolment, attendance, fees, or our legal duties. Making an account inactive stops sign-in. It does not always delete older school records.


13. Children

Student records are school records about children. The school creates them. They are shown to the parent account the school assigns, or to the parent who links an unassigned student with the correct roll number and date of birth.

Add health information only where the school needs it to care for the student.


14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top and publish the new text in Settings in the parent app and the teacher app.
